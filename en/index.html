What is DPLL?

DPLL (Davis–Putnam–Logemann–Loveland), which we call DLL here, is the classic computational logic algorithm for deciding the satisfiability (SAT) of a set of clauses: it combines unit propagation with branching, and it remains the basis of modern SAT solvers.
